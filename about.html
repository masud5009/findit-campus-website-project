Our idea

Little things matter.

Students move between classes, labs, the library, campus buses, and university events every day. In the rush, it is easy to leave something behind.

FindIt Campus presents a central place to browse found-item notices or create a simple report. The goal is to encourage a helpful, responsible campus community.

This is a front-end website design project created using HTML and CSS. The listings and form are examples for demonstration only.
